3. Demo Video
   - Public YouTube / Vimeo / Google Drive link

4. Category Specification
   - Clearly state: Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS TO HIGHLIGHT IN DECK
-----------------------------------------
- Multi-persona AI panel (not a single chatbot) — each persona has distinct goals
- Fact-Checker uses Google Search grounding (live web search during session)
- Condition tracking as a structured progress metric across the session
- Gemini vision API for scanned PDF extraction
- Coaching report quotes specific turns and suggests better answers
- Multiple industry verticals: Business, Event, Academic, Startup, Policy
- Google Calendar sync + "link to meeting" — rehearsal tied to a real upcoming meeting


NOTES FROM ANALYSIS (2026-10-15)
