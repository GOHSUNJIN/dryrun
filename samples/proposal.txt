PROJECT PROPOSAL: AI-Powered Employee Onboarding Assistant ("OnboardAI")
Submitted by: People & Culture Team
Date: October 2026

─────────────────────────────────────────────
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────
We propose building an internal AI assistant that guides new hires through
their first 90 days. The assistant answers HR policy questions, schedules
introductory meetings, assigns and tracks onboarding tasks, and gives managers
a live progress dashboard.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PROBLEM
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Our current onboarding process relies on a 40-page PDF handbook and ad-hoc
Slack messages from HR. New hires take an average of 6 weeks to reach full
productivity. Exit surveys show that 23% of early attrition cites "feeling
lost in the first month" as a contributing factor.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PROPOSED SOLUTION
─────────────────────────────────────────────
OnboardAI will be a conversational interface integrated into our existing
Slack workspace. Using a large language model fine-tuned on our internal HR
documentation, it will:

  • Answer policy and benefits questions 24/7
  • Auto-generate a personalised 90-day task plan per role and department
  • Send daily check-in prompts and surface blockers to the manager dashboard
  • Integrate with our HRIS (Workday) and calendar system (Google Workspace)

─────────────────────────────────────────────
FINANCIALS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Estimated build cost:    $120,000
  (6-month project: 3 engineers + 1 PM)
Estimated annual saving: $200,000
  (reduced HR hours + lower first-year attrition cost)
Payback period:          approximately 7 months

Note: vendor and infrastructure costs are not yet included in these figures
pending vendor selection.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
TIMELINE
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Month 1–2  Requirements gathering, vendor evaluation, data preparation
Month 3–4  Development and internal QA
Month 5    Pilot with 50 new hires across two departments
Month 6    Company-wide rollout

─────────────────────────────────────────────
RISKS (IDENTIFIED)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
